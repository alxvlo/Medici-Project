# Asset List 03 — Backgrounds

**Date:** 7 September 2026 · **5 files** · One of eight lists; see `00-index.md`
**Who draws it:** Me. Rooms are the easiest thing to generate convincingly and the least identifying
— nobody will look at a waiting room and think of your character sheet. Take any of them back if you
want to.

---

## Before you start

**Style.** Soft cel-shading, dark-brown outlines, muted earthy colours, flat lighting, no heavy
gradients. Backgrounds may be a little more painterly than the characters, but stay in the same
palette and stay **lower in contrast than the characters** — a patient has to read instantly
against any of these.

**Format.** PNG. **These five may be opaque** — they are the only assets that need no transparency.
**Draw at twice the size listed:** 1920 × 1280 files. Name each file exactly as shown, lowercase, no
spaces.

The game screen is 960 × 640, landscape, and these fill it edge to edge.

---

## What these are for

Five rooms, one per phase of a case. The student moves through them in this order, and the whole
game happens on top of them, so **where you leave empty space matters more than the detail you put
in.** Each note below says which part of the frame the game needs kept clear.

## The list

| ✓ | File name | Size | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | `bg-title.png` | 960 × 640 | Exterior or lobby of a small clinic, warm and welcoming. **Leave the centre-left third fairly plain** so the logo and menu buttons can sit on it. |
| ☐ | `bg-reception.png` | 960 × 640 | Reception / waiting area where the patient walks in. A doorway on the left, a counter on the right. **The patient stands centre-left and their speech bubble goes above them** — keep that area uncluttered. |

| ☐ | `bg-console.png` | 960 × 640 | The control console, close-up, seen from the operator's seat. **Leave a flat panel area across the centre** — the kVp and mAs dials sit there, and later the two collimation knobs. |
| ☐ | `bg-viewer.png` | 960 × 640 | A darkened reading room with a large lightbox or monitor in the centre where the X-ray appears. **Leave the centre clear.** This is also where the end-of-case review is shown, so a calm, low-contrast room works best. |

If any of these rooms is wrong for how your school actually looks — the wall stand on the other side,
a different console layout — tell me, because that is the one thing I cannot generate my way out of.
