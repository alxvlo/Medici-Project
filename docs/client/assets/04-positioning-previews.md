## Before you start

**Style.** Match your character sheet: soft cel-shading, dark-brown outlines, muted earthy colours, flat lighting, no heavy gradients.

**Format.** PNG, transparent background. Square. **Draw at twice the size listed** — send 480 × 480 files. Name each file exactly as shown, lowercase, no spaces; a typo means the game shows a grey box instead.

The game screen is 960 × 640, landscape.

---

## What these are for

**This is the most important list after the films.** Three per level: one correct position and two wrong ones, shown side by side as buttons. The student picks one, gets **a single attempt**, and if they pick wrong the game explains why that position fails and then shows them the correct one.

That makes these pictures the question itself. The student has to be able to tell the three apart at a glance and reason about the difference — so the thing that distinguishes each position (the angle of the foot, whether the palm is up or down, whether the chin is raised) needs to be the clearest thing in the drawing.

They appear small as buttons, about 240 × 240, and enlarge when the student hovers or holds one. **Keep them readable at both sizes.** Use the patient for that level, on the table or at the wall stand.
