    ## Before you start

    **Style.** Match your character sheet: soft cel-shading, dark-brown outlines, muted earthy colours, flat lighting, no heavy gradients.

    **Format.** PNG, transparent background. **Draw at twice the size listed** — "300 × 600" means send a 600 × 1200 file. Name each file exactly as shown, lowercase, no spaces; a typo means the game shows a grey box instead. Sizes are guides — only the aspect ratio matters much.

    The game screen is 960 × 640, landscape.

    ---

    ## What these are for

    Six patients — one per level, plus a spare — taken from your character sheet. Each needs **two versions**: standing normally, and the same pose with a pained expression and a hand on the injury.

    The idle version appears when the patient walks into reception at the start of a case. The pain version replaces it while they speak their line, so the student's first clue about the injury is visual. Front-facing, feet visible, as on your sheet.
