## Before you start

**Style.** Match your character sheet: soft cel-shading, dark-brown outlines, muted earthy colours,
flat lighting, no heavy gradients.

**Format.** PNG, transparent background. **Draw at twice the size listed** — "300 × 300" means a
600 × 600 file. Name each file exactly as shown, lowercase, no spaces.

The game screen is 960 × 640, landscape.

---

## What these are for

The student *is* the radiologic technologist, so we never show them as a full character — only their
hand, at the one moment it matters.

That moment is the exposure. Having set the technique and the collimation, the student presses and
**holds** the exposure button: the tube spins up, the exposure fires, and only then does the X-ray
appear. The two pictures are the up and down states of that button, and they carry the whole beat,
so the pressed version needs to read clearly as *pressed* — the finger visibly down, the button sunk.
