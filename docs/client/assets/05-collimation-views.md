## Before you start

**Style.** Match your character sheet: soft cel-shading, dark-brown outlines, muted earthy colours, flat lighting, no heavy gradients.

**Format.** PNG, transparent background. Square. **Draw at twice the size listed** — send 960 × 960 files. Name each file exactly as shown, lowercase, no spaces; a typo means the game shows a grey box instead.

The game screen is 960 × 640, landscape.

---

## What these are for

One per level. The same body region as the positioning step, but now **already positioned** on the table or at the wall stand, and seen **from the tube's point of view** — straight down for anything on the table, straight on for anything at the wall stand.

The game draws a translucent collimation light rectangle over the top of this, plus a red target marker, and the student turns two knobs to size the light field to the target. So the picture needs to sit still and read clearly underneath a lit rectangle: **even lighting, no strong highlights, and the anatomy filling most of the frame.**

The student may keep adjusting until the field is right, so this is the one picture they will look at for a while.
