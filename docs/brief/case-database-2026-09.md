- **Level 7** gives `mAs: 3-5` with no unit, where every other case writes `mAs`.
- **Level 9** positioning reads `TIbia-Fibula AP`, with a capital I.

## Gaps against what the game needs

**Present and ready to use** for all twenty cases: patient identity, chief complaint, history,
provisional diagnosis, doctor's order, requested projection, pathology, correct kVp, correct mAs, and
a collimation instruction. Eleven of twenty also carry a working reference link for the correct film.

**Still needed from the client:**

1. **Two wrong positioning options per level, with one sentence each on why they are wrong.** The
   database gives only the correct projection. This is the entire positioning stage.
2. **Tolerance bands and dial limits for kVp and mAs.** Nine cases give a single value and eleven
   give a range, and it is not stated whether a range is the acceptable band or the client's own
   uncertainty about the right value.
3. **Collimation as a measurable field size.** Every entry is prose, such as "to area of lung fields",
   which does not convert to an on-screen target without a dimension.
4. **Debrief text** — what the underexposed film and the overexposed film each fail to show.
5. **The patient's opening line of dialogue.**
6. **Structures that must be visible** for eighteen of the twenty cases. Only levels 10 and 11 have
   it, marked `SS:` in the source.
7. **Films.** The client has confirmed they will supply the overexposed and underexposed films.
   Nine levels also lack a usable correct-image reference: 7, 8, 9, 13, 17, and 18 have none at all;
   10 and 11 have a caption but no link; and 12's link text is present while its URL is not.

**Two internal conflicts for the client to resolve:**

- **Level 15** requests AP Skull but is positioned PA Caldwell.
- **Level 19** requests AP Erect but is positioned AP Abdomen with no erect qualifier.
